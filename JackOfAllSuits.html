👑 Jack of all Suits 👑

✨ Introduction and Game Setup (let's get this bread!) ✨

This game is an adaptation of the card game 500, but like... way simpler and cooler! It's giving "easy to learn but will ruin friendships" energy! 💅

Made from a deck of 54 cards (2 jokers added because chaos is fun!) This game is suitable for 2 - 8 players - squad goals fr fr! 🔥

Before the first round starts, each player agrees on an acceptable number of cards to have (this number must be even, usually following the standard card amounts per category). The amount of cards decided will then be dealt to each player for the game to start! 💀

Each player also starts with 1000 points to use during the game (these points can instead be money if you're feeling spicy! 💸)

🎮 How to Play (it's giving tutorial!) 🎮

The player who dealt the cards will be known as "The Dealer" - this role will move clockwise after each round unless a player relinquishes it to the next player (big brain move! 🧠). The Dealer chooses a suit to be known as the "Round Suit". This suit will determine the value of each card! ✨

Starting at the dealer, each player will place a singular card face-down with the intent of placing the best card for the round. High stakes bestie! 💀

Once all players have placed their card face-down, the bidding begins! 🔥

Players will go round adding bids until each player still playing has an equal amount of bids in the pot. If a player decides they don't want to match a bid they can instead choose to "fold" and remove themselves from the round (their bid remains in the pot tho - rip!).

💰 Bidding (money moves!) 💰

Once the bidding has concluded, the round will then start with the dealer revealing their card first! 👀

The player who placed the highest card of the round suit will win the round and take the pot! If no player placed a card of the round suit then the highest card of any suit will win the round. 💅

The winner takes the pot and the next round will begin with the dealer changing to the next player clockwise (if they wish to take the role of dealer bestie!). ✨

📊 Card Values (the hierarchy hits different!) 📊

The card rankings in this game are ranked as follows from highest to lowest (study this like it's finals week! 📚):

  • Jack of the trump suit (THE GOAT! 🐐)
  • Jack of the same colour as the trump suit
  • Ace of the trump suit
  • 10 of the trump suit
  • King of the trump suit
  • Queen of the trump suit
  • 9 of the trump suit

  • 4 of the trump suit

  • 2 of the trump suit

  • Ace of non-trump suits (in order: Clubs, Hearts, Diamonds, Spades)
  • 10 of non-trump suits (in order: Clubs, Hearts, Diamonds, Spades)
