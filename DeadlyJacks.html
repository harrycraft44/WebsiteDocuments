📊 Card Values (the hierarchy hits different!) 📊

The card rankings in this game are ranked as follows from highest to lowest (the Jacks said "we run this game now"! 👑):

  • Jack of Suits Dominant (2 Jacks of same colour and suit colour) - THE ULTIMATE COMBO! 🔥
  • Jack of Suits Subordinate (2 Jacks of same colour but not suit colour)
  • Joker (can be paired with any card - the wildcard bestie!) 🃏
  • Royal Flush (2 consecutive cards of the same suit and both Royals) 👑
  • Straight Flush (2 consecutive cards of the same suit)
  • Two of a kind / Pair (2 cards of the same rank)
  • Straight (2 consecutive cards of different suits)
  • Flush (2 cards of the same suit)
  • High Card (the highest card played) (A,K,Q,J,10,9,8,7,6,5,4,3,2) - the participation trophy of hands! 💀
