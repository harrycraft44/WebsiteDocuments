👻 The Final Hour at the Count's Manor 👻

This one-shot is serving haunted mansion realness bestie! 🏚️ You can take a comedic spin on this or go full horror - the choice is yours fr fr! With mind-bending puzzles that'll make your brain hurt (in a good way!), intense battles that slap, and confusing mazes that are lowkey giving "I'm lost but it's aesthetic" - this One-Shot is sure to immerse your players in a spooky atmosphere! 💀✨

This game will consist of two puzzles, 1 fight scene, and a maze before the epic throwdown at the end against The Count - a powerful vampire with secrets hidden around his lair! 🧛✨

🎮 Game Setup (it's giving DM prep!) 🎮

The players could start off in the back of a cart being taken to the manor and forced to go in there (what I did - no consent was given lmao 💀), however there are other ways you could start this adventure such as:

  • Brave adventurers who want to explore the unknown (main character energy! ✨)
  • Kidnapped prisoners forced to enter the mansion (trauma bonding speedrun! 😭)
